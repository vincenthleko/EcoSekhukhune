Knowledge creates change

Know what goes where

Start with these basic recycling guidelines.

🥤

Plastic

Separate recyclable plastic containers and packaging. Empty and rinse them when practical, and check local collection rules.

📦

Paper and cardboard

Keep recyclable paper dry and clean. Remove excessive food residue and separate materials according to local requirements.

🍾

Glass and metal

Keep bottles, jars and metal cans separate where accepted. Handle broken glass carefully to prevent injuries.

🍂

Organic waste

Fruit and vegetable scraps may be suitable for composting. Keep plastics and other non-compostable materials out.

💻

Electronic waste

Keep old electronics, cables and accessories separate from ordinary rubbish. Use an appropriate e-waste collection service.

🤝

Start locally

Organise a clean-up, encourage responsible disposal, and ask your municipality about local recycling services and requirements.
